PLANKED

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026-10-08. Replace kertlegaming@gmail.com before publishing.

PLANKED ("the app") is a game. We collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device. Your scores, settings, gold, gems, unlocked ships and sails, and mission progress are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them.

What we process. When you buy gems, Apple processes the payment. Our server receives the signed transaction from Apple and a random identifier generated by the app (not your name, Apple ID or email) so we can credit your gems once and handle refunds. We keep: that identifier, the transaction ID, the product bought, and the date.

What we do not do. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no accounts, and no information collected from children.

Payments. Handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Retention and deletion. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to honour purchases and refunds. To ask for deletion, email kertlegaming@gmail.com with the date of purchase.

Children. The app is suitable for ages 9+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes. We will update this page if this policy changes.

Contact: kertlegaming@gmail.com
