Common questions

I bought gems but they haven't arrived.
Open PLANKED while online. Unfinished purchases are credited automatically. Still missing? Email us your Apple receipt.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Does the game need internet?
No. Only gem purchases need a connection.
How do I earn gems for free?
Collect them on the water, claim Daily Treasure and finish daily missions.
Controls?
Swipe left/right to steer, swipe up or tap to hop, swipe down to brake.
